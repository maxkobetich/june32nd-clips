Privacy Policy

1. Data the app accesses

Through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, the app can access:

  • Basic TikTok account information (username, profile), used only to confirm the connected account.
  • Video upload permission, used only to upload the owner's videos to his own TikTok drafts.

2. How data is used

Account information and access tokens are used solely to operate the app's single function: uploading the owner's videos to his own TikTok drafts. The app performs no analytics, serves no ads, and shares no data with any third party.

3. Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely on the owner's private equipment. No account data is stored on any public server.

4. No other users

The app has no public users. It collects no personal data from anyone other than the owner's own TikTok account, as authorized by the owner through TikTok's login flow.
